Common Mistakes to Avoid

Skipping the review, approving AI output without reading it line by line. You signed it; you're responsible for it.

Wrong tag order, Assisted-by comes before your Signed-off-by in the trailer block.

Listing basic tools, do not list git, gcc, or your editor. Only specialized analysis tools belong in Assisted-by.

Wrong model version, use the specific model identifier, not a marketing name. claude-sonnet-4-6, not "Claude Sonnet".

Assuming AI output is license-clean, AI models are trained on vast corpora. Always verify that the generated code doesn't inadvertently reproduce incompatibly-licensed snippets.

Omitting Assisted-by entirely, the doc says "should include" (not "must"), but disclosure is the intent. Omitting it is technically allowed for now, but signals poor practice.
