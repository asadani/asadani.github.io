If you only remember three things.

Contextual retrieval is good packaging of known parts: contextualized embeddings + hybrid search + rerank, made cheap by prompt caching. The packaging is real value; it is not a research breakthrough.

The reported gains are real but bounded. Up to ~67% fewer failed retrievals on Anthropic's evals. Your corpus, query distribution, and evaluation method decide whether you see anywhere near that.

It does not fix the systemic problems, context blindness, agentic reasoning, structured grounding, and content drift. Build evals and query reformulation first; layer contextual retrieval on top, not in place.
