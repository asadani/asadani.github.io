# Writing and publishing references

The canonical references live in the repository and should be read fresh rather than duplicated here.

## Required

- `CLAUDE.md` — current design system and publishing rules.
- `_template/article.html` — canonical article skeleton.

## Default worked example

- `spec-lies-until-something-runs-it/index.html`

This article is the fallback worked example because it explicitly captures an important lesson of this workflow: template + procedure alone can preserve grammar but still miss how a finished article hangs together. Read a real finished article before producing a new one.

## Choosing a better example

If the new piece resembles another recent article more closely, use that article instead or alongside the fallback. Choose by content shape, not just topic keywords:
- taxonomy / framework;
- technical experiment;
- argument / critique;
- architecture / system map;
- product or market analysis.

Examples are references for composition and voice, not text to copy.
