What this means for engineers shipping in 2026.

Four pieces of practical advice for anyone building agentic systems this year.

Build self-improvement loops where you have a clean verifier. Code agents with passing tests. Schema-validation with explicit grammar. Numeric optimization with measurable outputs. These ship safely.

Do not ship self-improvement where reward is contested. Customer-facing summarization. Open-ended chat quality. Anything where the grader is itself a model whose disagreement rate with humans isn't measured. The loop will look like it works, then drift.

Treat scaffold versioning as first-class infrastructure. If your harness is changing autonomously, you need diffable history per generation, attribution between harness and weight gains, and independent rollback of each lever. Without that, a regression three generations in is functionally unrecoverable.

Run a frozen out-of-distribution eval every generation. A held-out, perturbation-rich evaluation set you do not optimize against. This is your early warning that the coupled Goodhart equilibrium is drifting away from V.

None of this is AGI strategy. It is engineering advice that maps to what the field can actually deliver in 2026, not to what the phrase "self-improving AI" tends to promise on a slide.
