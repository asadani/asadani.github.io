Ways this goes wrong.

Most "data product" initiatives that stall do so for predictable reasons. None of them are technical. Watch for these and push back early.

Cosmetic relabeling. Renaming existing tables to "products" without contracts, SLAs, or owners, all of the overhead, none of the benefit.

Mesh without platform. Handing ownership to domains before the platform team has built the self-serve substrate, every domain reinvents observability badly.

Governance as gate. Routing every new product through a central review committee. The point of governance is to scale without that committee.

Contract rot. Contracts that live in wiki pages instead of code diverge from reality in weeks. If it isn't reviewed in a PR, it isn't a contract.

Output port sprawl. Exposing every product via every possible port "just in case." Ports are a commitment, each one is a long-term SLA.

Silent quality. Quality checks that log but don't block or page. A failing check nobody sees is worse than no check at all.
