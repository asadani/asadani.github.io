If you only remember three things.

Prompt optimization is a stack, not a tool. Search + evals + observability + governance, layered. Picking one product and calling it done is the most common failure.

Your evaluator is your real product. Goodhart's Law makes the metric the attack surface, defend it with structured rubrics, position-rotated LLM judges, and dynamic eval scenarios, not a single scalar score.

The frameworks that win are the ones that take a specific layer seriously: DSPy compiles pipelines, TextGrad attributes failures, ACE curates context, GEPA evolves under multi-objective constraints, Bedrock and Humanloop govern lifecycle. Use them for what they are. Don't ask any of them to be the whole stack.
