The short version.

A 4 GB card from 2019 runs a 0.6B model at four times CPU speed. Getting there was almost entirely plumbing, and almost every failure was silent.

If you have an old GPU sitting in a laptop, it is probably more capable than the last time you checked, because the models came down to meet it.

Update the driver before you debug anything else.

Check torch.version.cuda after every install that touches torch.

Confirm your architecture is in get_arch_list(). Newer CUDA drops old cards.

On Windows, put the CUDA DLL directories on PATH, not just add_dll_directory.

Use bf16 on pre-Ampere hardware even though fp16 is native. Correctness first.

Tune batch size against peak VRAM. The clock will mislead you.

Read the Windows event log before theorising about a crash.

Cache work to disk. Long local jobs get interrupted for reasons you cannot control.
